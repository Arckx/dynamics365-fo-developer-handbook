## 2. Exam Domain Focus Areas

### MB-310: Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance Functional Consultant
For Finance consultants, the handbook provides deep technical context for core functional configurations:
*   **Core Setup (Ch 9, 10, 15)**: Understanding how tables, forms, and security underpin the financial ledger.
*   **Business Processes (Ch 13, 29)**: Mastering the workflow engine for journal approvals and financial routing.
*   **Reporting (Ch 17)**: Technical understanding of SSRS to design and troubleshoot financial reports.

### MB-330: Microsoft Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management Functional Consultant
For SCM consultants, the handbook bridges the gap between functional requirements and technical implementation:
*   **Data Integrity (Ch 9, 11)**: Understanding table relations and lookups for item and inventory management.
*   **Integration (Ch 16, 25)**: Mastering how data entities and Power Platform connect D365 to external supply chain tools.
*   **Security & Compliance (Ch 15, 28)**: Implementing secure access for warehouse and procurement operations.

### MB-500: Microsoft Dynamics 365: Finance and Operations Apps Developer
This handbook is the primary study resource for the MB-500 developer certification:
*   **The Data Layer (Ch 1-3, 9, 11)**: Deep understanding of the AOT and model store architecture.
*   **Programming (Ch 5-7, 12-14, 23, 35)**: Mastering X++ from basics to advanced service patterns and JSON interop.
*   **Extensibility (Ch 14)**: The most critical exam topic — mastering CoC and Event Handlers.
*   **DevOps & Lifecycle (Ch 4, 19, 20, 27)**: Building pipelines, managing environments in LCS, and automated testing with SysTest.
*   **Performance (Ch 21, 24)**: SQL optimization and diagnostic tools (Trace Parser).
