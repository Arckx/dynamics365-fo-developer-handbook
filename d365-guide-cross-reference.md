## Curriculum Phases (Recommended Progression)

1. **Quarter 1 (Foundations):** Ch 1–9. Focus on architecture, model, VS setup, X++ types, control flow, methods, tables.
2. **Quarter 2 (Extensibility):** Ch 10–15, 29, 31, 32. Focus on CoC, events, tables/forms extensibility, security, workflow.
3. **Quarter 3 (Integration & Advanced):** Ch 16, 17, 19, 23, 24, 30, 35. Focus on entities, DMF, SSRS, testing, debugging, number sequences, services.
4. **Quarter 4 (DevOps & Production):** Ch 4, 18, 20–22, 25–28, 33, 34. Focus on LCS, performance, batch, module specialization, compliance, DevOps.
